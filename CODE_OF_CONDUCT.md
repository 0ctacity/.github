# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Octacity is committed to providing a respectful, constructive, and welcoming environment for everyone who participates in the community.

This Code of Conduct applies to all Octacity spaces, including repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, meetings, interviews, events, and official communication channels.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Treat others with respect and consideration.
- Communicate constructively, even during disagreement.
- Give and receive feedback in good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas, decisions, and work rather than individuals.
- Respect different levels of experience, backgrounds, and perspectives.
- Ask questions when expectations or decisions are unclear.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and make reasonable efforts to correct them.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person’s private information without permission.
- Follow project-specific contribution and communication guidelines.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insults, threats, hostile language, or repeated disruptive behavior.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Deliberate exclusion or humiliation of another participant.
- Publishing or threatening to publish private information without permission.
- Impersonation, deliberate misrepresentation, or deceptive conduct.
- Spamming, trolling, or repeatedly disrupting productive discussion.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.
- Using community access, authority, or resources to pressure or exploit others.

## Technical Disagreement

Disagreement is a normal part of collaborative work.

Participants should explain their reasoning, provide evidence when possible, and remain open to alternative approaches. Technical criticism should be specific, relevant, and directed at the work.

Maintainers may close, moderate, or redirect discussions that become repetitive, hostile, unrelated, or unproductive.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies when a person is:

- Participating in an Octacity repository or project.
- Communicating through an official Octacity channel.
- Representing Octacity publicly.
- Attending an Octacity meeting, interview, event, or collaboration session.
- Interacting with others in connection with Octacity activities.

Conduct outside official spaces may also be considered when it creates a clear risk to the safety, trust, or continued participation of community members.

## Reporting

Concerns should be reported privately to the designated Octacity contact.

Reports should include, when possible:

- A description of what happened.
- Where and when it occurred.
- Relevant links, messages, or screenshots.
- The names or usernames of the people involved.
- Any immediate safety or confidentiality concerns.

Do not use public issues or discussions to report sensitive conduct concerns.

Reports will be reviewed as privately and fairly as reasonably possible. Information will only be shared with people who need it to review or address the situation.

> A private reporting method will be added here before community applications are opened.

## Enforcement

The Octacity founder and authorized community leaders are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Responses may include:

1. A private clarification or warning.
2. Removal or moderation of content.
3. Temporary restrictions on participation.
4. Removal from a project or team.
5. Removal from the Octacity organization.
6. A permanent ban from Octacity spaces.

The response will depend on the seriousness, context, impact, and history of the behavior.

Severe conduct may result in immediate removal without a prior warning.

## Good-Faith Reports

Reports made honestly and in good faith will not result in retaliation.

Knowingly false, misleading, or malicious reports may themselves be treated as a violation of this Code of Conduct.

## Appeals

A person affected by an enforcement decision may request reconsideration by responding through the private communication channel provided with the decision.

An appeal should explain why the decision may have been based on incomplete information, a misunderstanding, or a disproportionate response. Appeals do not guarantee that a decision will be changed.

## Responsibility

Participation in Octacity means agreeing to follow this Code of Conduct and helping maintain a healthy environment for others.

Community access is based not only on technical contribution, but also on responsible and respectful participation.
